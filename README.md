# Yogesh Honamore — Digital Marketing Portfolio

A hand-made, calm, and professional single-page digital marketing portfolio designed for HR managers and growth hiring leads.

---

## 🎨 Visual System & Direction

- **Exact Palette**:
  - Light mode: Background `#f3f1ea`, Text `#13121a`, Muted `#6b6972`, Lines `#d6d3c8`, Accent Red `#ff4b2b`, Yellow `#ffd84d` (used strictly for tool-pill hover), Card `#fffefa`.
  - Dark mode: Background `#14131a`, Text `#f3f1ea`, Muted `#9c9aa3`, Lines `#34323c`, Accent Red `#ff4b2b`.
  - Zero gradients, glassmorphism, glows, or stock illustrations.
- **Exact Typography**:
  - Headings & numbers: `Bricolage Grotesque` (weights 500 & 800) with tight letter spacing (`-0.03em`).
  - Body copy: `Instrument Sans` (weights 400 & 600).
- **Universal Easing**: `cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1)` for all micro-interactions.
- **Interactive Background**: Full-screen canvas grid of tiny dashes with subtle idle sine-wave tilt and mouse-following red accent swing (with power-saving tab visibility pauses and `prefers-reduced-motion` still mode).

---

## 📐 Page Structure

1. **Top Bar**: Display name on the left, green dot with "Open to work" on the right (not sticky).
2. **Hero**: Headline (*"I turn flat lines into growth charts."*) with word-by-word mask reveal and 3px red underline under *"growth charts."*, followed by a muted lead sentence.
3. **Content Sections** (Two-column layout, 200px label left, content right, 1.5px dark line above):
   - **Selected Work**: Result numbers counting up from 0, Problem / What I did / Result lines appearing sequentially, 8px right shift on hover.
   - **Tools**: Outlined pill tags that turn yellow (`#ffd84d`) and tilt `-1.5deg` on hover.
   - **How I Think**: Large statement (*"Know the buyer. Test small. Keep what works."*).
   - **Contact**: Display *"Say hello."* with dark pill button.
4. **Footer**:
   - Left: *"What's next?"* label, large closing line (*"Got a role? Let's talk."*) with animated red underline, dark *"Email me →"* and outlined *"Copy email"* (with 1.8s *"Copied ✓"* feedback).
   - Right: Link rows (*LinkedIn ↗*, *Resume ↓*, *[PLACEHOLDER: other profile] ↗*) with 10px hover shift and red underline.
   - Bottom Strip: Live local time auto-updating every 30 seconds, auto-updating copyright year, and *"↑ Back to top"*.
